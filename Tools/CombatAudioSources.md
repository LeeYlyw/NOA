# Player pain voice source

`SourceAudio/Grunts_aphexx_347337_hq.mp3` is the high-quality public preview of
“SFX_Voice_Grunts_Male_Close_MidLength_1.wav” by aphexx_.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/aphexx_/sounds/347337/
- License: CC0, as stated on the source page (checked 2026-11-24)
- Edits: two short grunts cut from 0.64–0.94 s and 2.22–2.52 s, converted to
  48 kHz mono PCM WAV, high-pass filtered at 80 Hz, faded at both ends, and normalized.
- To regenerate: install `miniaudio`, then run `python Tools/ExtractPlayerPain.py`.
